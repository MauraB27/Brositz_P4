
Project 4: Bread Barnstorm\
![Godot Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/Godot-4.x-blue?logo=godot-engine&logoColor=white)
![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-PC%20/%20Web-green)

Developed by:\
Maura Brositz: Lead Programmer and Level Designer

Brain Barnstorm offers players a chance to travel as a piece of bread throughout various locations such as shops and hideaways  in a forest. Players must collect items and solve puzzles in order to unlock secret doors to find their way home and out of the forest.
---
How to Play\
[Play in Browser](https://sunsett556.itch.io/bread-barnstorm)\
password: kawaiipan26

Solve the puzzles by unlocking doors and going through mazes. Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys
